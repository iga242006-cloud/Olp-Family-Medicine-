Our story

Meet Dr. Olp

She founded this practice because of how it felt to be a patient

Before she was the doctor Carmel families text on a Saturday morning, Dr. Ashlie Olp was a patient — sitting in a waiting room, watching the clock, and leaving an appointment feeling more like a billing code than a person. That frustrating experience never left her. In 2017, it became the reason she opened her own practice.

A Carmel native, Dr. Olp earned her medical degree at the Indiana University School of Medicine and completed her residency at Franciscan Health Indianapolis. She is a board-certified family physician with special focus in family medicine, women's health, fitness and sports medicine, and diabetes care.

She built Olp Family Medicine on a simple promise: her patients would get the access, advocacy, and thoughtful care she once wished she'd had. That's why the practice is membership-based, why her patient panel stays intentionally small, and why appointments run 30–60 minutes instead of seven.

First female winner of the Carmel Marathon 10+ marathons and counting — she practices the endurance she preaches.

That drive shows up everywhere: in how she coaches members through weight loss and training goals, in how she digs into hormone health when other doctors shrug, and in how she answers her patients' texts herself.

The team

Small team. Deep relationships.

When you call, text, or walk in, you're greeted by people who know you — not a rotating cast of strangers.

M

Molly

Nurse Practitioner · Adult Primary Care

Molly joined the practice in 2021 and cares for adult primary care patients with the same unhurried, personal approach the practice was founded on. A graduate of the Vanderbilt School of Nursing, she previously served as a registered nurse at Ascension St. Vincent, where she built her reputation for meeting patients with skill and genuine warmth.

C

Candi

Professional Medical Assistant

Candi has been a professional medical assistant since 1992 — and has worked alongside Dr. Olp since 2002. For more than two decades of patients, Candi has been the familiar face and steady hand of every visit. She's often the first person members see, and one of the biggest reasons they stay.
