Direct Primary Care, explained

Simpler than insurance. Better because of it.

Direct Primary Care (DPC) removes the insurance middleman from your relationship with your doctor. Here's how it works in three steps.

Join for one flat monthly fee

Your membership is your payment — like a gym for your health, but with a physician who knows your name. No claims, no codes, no copays.

Get unlimited primary care & direct access

Visits, labs, virtual care, texting your doctor — it's all included. Because no insurer sets the schedule, visits stay long and access stays fast.

Keep insurance for the big stuff

Many members pair DPC with a high-deductible or catastrophic plan for emergencies, hospital care, and specialists — often spending less overall.

Common questions

Thinking it over?

Do you take insurance?

No — and that's by design. Olp Family Medicine doesn't bill insurance for your care, which is what makes long visits, same-day access, and transparent pricing possible. Many members pair their membership with a high-deductible or catastrophic plan for emergencies, hospital care, and specialists.

Is membership like insurance?

No. Membership covers your primary care directly at the practice — it isn't an insurance product and doesn't cover hospitalization or specialists. Think of it as having a family doctor on retainer: unlimited primary care, one flat monthly fee.

What if I need care outside of primary care?

Dr. Olp coordinates your care and advocates for you — referring you to trusted specialists when needed and helping you navigate the system. That advocacy is a core part of the practice's mission.
