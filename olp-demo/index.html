Why membership

Primary care the way it was meant to feel

No waiting rooms full of strangers. No seven-minute visits. Just a small patient panel, real relationships, and a physician who knows your story.

Same or next-day visits

When you're sick, you're seen — usually the same day. No triage lines, no "first available in three weeks."

30–60 minute appointments

Time to actually talk — about symptoms, stress, goals, and everything in between. You'll never feel rushed out the door.

Call, text, or video your doctor

Members reach Dr. Olp and the team directly — no phone trees, no portals that go nowhere. Just answers.

One transparent monthly fee

No insurance billing. No copays. No surprise statements six weeks later. You always know exactly what care costs.

Annual labs included

Preventative services and your annual labs are part of membership — because staying well shouldn't cost extra.

A small patient panel, on purpose

Dr. Olp limits how many patients she cares for so every member gets the access and attention they're paying for.

Transparent pricing

One simple monthly fee. That's it.

Your membership covers all primary care, preventative services, annual labs, and virtual visits — with no copays and no insurance paperwork, ever.
